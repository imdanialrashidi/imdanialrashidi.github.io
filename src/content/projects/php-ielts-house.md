PHP IELTS House is an English and IELTS institute in Karaj. I designed and implemented its website in 2024. The site has a narrow job: explain what the institute teaches, show the results it publishes, and make a free placement test or a question easy to act on.

I designed for people arriving on a phone, often between other things, with one question in mind: which class fits me. The site needed a clear answer path rather than atmosphere, so the information a prospective student needs sits close to the surface.

## The brief

The brief was to make the institute legible. A learner needs to see the teaching approach, the course levels, the published results and the next step, in that order, without decoding a menu. I kept the structure simpler than a typical school site, with nothing decorative competing with the copy. I assumed a phone screen first, so clear information mattered more than ornament.

## What I built

The homepage opens with a photo of two students working at a table, a short line naming the institute as a specialised IELTS and conversation centre in Karaj, and its own headline about a fast route to IELTS 7. Two buttons sit below that copy: one starts a free consultation and placement test, the other opens the students' results. The header repeats the placement action, and the navigation covers home, educational services, results, about PHP and the blog.

Course pages describe the levels and the time each path takes, as the institute states them: intermediate to IELTS 7 in eight months, and from zero to IELTS 7 in sixteen. The results page carries the band scores and testimonials the institute publishes. The site also lists supporting courses such as mock IELTS, intensive conversation and pre-IELTS, along with a free placement class, and explains the teaching approach: reading and listening practice at home, class time spent on speaking. Contact routes include phone, WhatsApp, Telegram and Instagram, an address in Mehrshahr, opening hours and a map link. A blog exists for pre-enrolment questions. The FAQ section on the live contact page still carries placeholder copy, so I do not count it as finished content.

## Limits and what I did not measure

My work was design and implementation; teaching, results and enrolment are the institute's work. I installed no analytics or lead tracking, so I have no traffic, conversion, booking or revenue figures for the site, and I do not estimate any. The band scores, course durations and testimonials on the site are content the institute supplied. I did not verify them independently, and they are not outcomes of the build. Hours, courses and contact details change, so check the live site before relying on a specific claim. The site's marketing language, including its claim to be the only specialised IELTS and conversation centre in Karaj, is the institute's own wording rather than a finding of this project.
