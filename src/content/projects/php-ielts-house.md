PHP IELTS House education/services site for specialised English and IELTS in Karaj — a presence to explain teaching approach and shorten the path to placement, consultation or enrolment. Public work; no independent enrolment/conversion metric is reported.

## Problem

The institute needed a coherent online presence that clarifies its teaching approach and courses and moves visitors toward placement, consultation or registration.

## What was built

- Homepage with "Specialised English & IELTS in Karaj" and placement/consultation CTAs
- Conversation-focused approach: per site copy, reading/listening practice at home, class time on speaking
- Course paths from intermediate to IELTS 7 (8 months) and zero to IELTS 7 (16 months) as stated on the site
- Student results and testimonial quotes (site content)
- Classroom environment, film/series analysis, interactive classes, mock IELTS, intensive conversation, pre-IELTS and free placement
- Blog, FAQ, contact/hours and map link

## Status

Public education site as listed on Noveno's portfolio. Independent enrolment or conversion data is not available; educational claims (scores, durations) are site-stated content and are not presented here as validated project metrics.

## Limitations

- Scores shown in testimonials and duration promises are not reported here as validated project results without independent source.
- No independent enrollment or conversion data is available in public sources.
