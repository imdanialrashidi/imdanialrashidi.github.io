Isbatab is the website for Imen Sanat Batab, a Tehran company that provides HSE consulting, safety training and technical inspection for industrial and construction projects. I designed and implemented the site in 2024. It presents the company to procurement managers, HSE officers and project owners who need to judge whether a contractor is qualified before they get in touch.

Industrial buyers read a safety contractor differently from a shop. They look for evidence: which services the company can actually staff, which standards it works to, which projects it has handled, and who to call. The company had that material. The website had to put it in one place, in a formal structure, with pages short enough to scan.

## The brief

The brief was a credibility problem before it was a sales one. The site needed to state what the company does in the first screen, back that up with services, certifications and project records, and keep a direct contact route visible on every page. The tone had to read as official without becoming stiff, and the layout had to stay calm. This is a company that inspects cranes and pressure vessels, so I kept the design closer to a technical dossier than to a startup landing page.

## What I built

The homepage opens with the company name and a short positioning statement covering oil, gas, petrochemical, mining and construction projects. Two direct actions sit under it: a call button with the sales mobile number and a WhatsApp request for cooperation. Below the hero are three cards for the points a buyer checks first: formally licensed services, HSE management system deployment with its documentation, and a specialist team with project experience.

The navigation has seven entries: home, about, services, certifications, projects, contact and blog. The services section covers HSE consulting, safety training, risk assessment and technical inspection of cranes, pressure vessels and fire systems. Related work includes HAZOP and HAZID studies, earthing and lightning protection, and engineering documentation such as as-built drawings, P&IDs, PFDs and 3D models. Certifications (ISO 45001, ISO 14001 and ISO 9001) have their own page, as do the project record, HSE policy and social responsibility. Phone and WhatsApp routes stay within reach throughout.

## Limits and what I did not measure

My work was design and implementation only. I installed no analytics or lead tracking, so I have no traffic, conversion, inquiry or revenue figures for the site, and I do not estimate any. The certifications, standards and project records shown on the site are the company's own material. They are evidence the company supplied, not outcomes of this build, and I did not audit them. Service scope, certificates and contact details change as the business changes, so check the live site before relying on a specific item. Whether the site helps the company win work is outside what a static brochure site can prove on its own.
