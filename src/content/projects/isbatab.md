Isbatab (Imen Sanat Batab) corporate/services site — a formal, trust-oriented web presence for HSE, safety, training and technical-inspection services. Presented as a designed site in Noveno's public portfolio; no lead or contract metrics are claimed.

## Problem

The company needed a credible online presence to present specialist services — HSE consulting, safety training, crane/forklift/machinery inspection, risk management, documentation and project résumé — to organisations and industrial clients.

## What was built

- Company introduction, history, team and HSE approach
- Safety and technical-inspection services (crane, forklift, machinery, structures)
- Consulting, HAZOP/HAZID, risk, building safety, earthing/lightning systems
- Training and HSE management-system implementation
- Engineering/documentation: As-Built, P&ID, PFD, 3D models
- Project résumé, certifications (ISO 45001, 14001, 9001), policy and social responsibility
- Phone and WhatsApp inquiry paths

## Status

Public corporate site; no validated lead, inquiry or contract data is available in public sources. Company-stated history (executive activity from 2013, formal registration/Tehran office from 2024) pertains to the company itself, not to the website build date.

## Limitations

- No validated lead/contract or conversion metric is reported.
- Certificates, standards and project records shown on the site belong to the company and are not presented here as outcomes of the website build.
