Elsa Hamrah is an online digital-goods store presented in Noveno's public portfolio. This profile stays strictly within what the public site shows — no sales, traffic or conversion numbers are claimed.

## Problem

The business needed an online path to present and sell phones, tablets, smartwatches and accessories with clear category browsing, brand filtering and trustworthy purchase information.

## What was built

- Online store for phones, tablets, smartwatches, headphones and accessories
- Brand filtering (e.g., Apple, Samsung, Xiaomi) and product search
- Order tracking, refund policy, terms, FAQ and support/contact
- Trust signals on the homepage: price guarantee, 24/7 support, return policy, authenticity and fast delivery

## Status

Public work listed as a designed site on Noveno's portfolio. At the time of the Noveno source review the site displayed "the store is currently closed for new orders" — re-check before citing availability. No validated sales/traffic/conversion data is available in public sources.

## Limitations

- No validated sales, visits or conversion metric is reported.
