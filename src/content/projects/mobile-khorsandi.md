Mobile Khorsandi is an online store for mobile phones, accessories, parts and gadgets — listed as a designed and implemented store in Noveno's public portfolio. No order or conversion numbers are claimed.

## Problem

The business needed an online store with clear categorisation for accessories, gadgets, phone parts and related goods — guiding shoppers quickly to the right product.

## What was built

- Store with shopping and cart path
- Categories including headphones/hands-free, chargers, cables, power banks, holders, microphones, adapters, watches, speakers, practical gadgets, phone protectors and phone parts
- Bestsellers, most-viewed and newest sections
- Search and product filtering, blog with tech news/buying guides/comparisons
- FAQ, warranty terms, about and contact/support pages
