Mobile Khorsandi is a mobile phone and accessories shop in Karaj. I designed and implemented its online store in 2024, and the work shipped that year. The shop sells phones, accessories, parts and small gadgets, and the website had one clear priority: help a shopper reach a product they understand, quickly.

A store like this lives on small items such as chargers, cases, cables and earbuds, and people often arrive knowing the shape of what they need but not the exact model. The site had to make that gap easy to cross.

## The brief

The brief was about shortening the path to the cart. Someone looking for a charger or a pair of earbuds should see the product type, recognise it and open a product page without reading a wall of categories first. I also planned a place for undecided shoppers: people comparing models or unsure what fits their phone. That meant buying guides beside the catalogue, not buried in a help centre.

## What I built

The header carries the shop's phone number, a search field, a login button and a cart with an item count. The navigation opens with a categories button, then shop, blog, frequently asked questions, warranty terms, contact and about. The homepage leads with a wide blue banner that names the shop and holds a "view store" button, plus three short badges: authenticity guarantee, express post delivery and responsive support. A repeating strip below lists fair pricing, countrywide express shipping, authenticity and support.

The category row shows product types as round image tiles: headphones and hands-free sets, chargers, cables, power banks, holders, microphones, adapters, watches, speakers, practical gadgets, phone protectors and phone parts. Further down, a bestseller section and a discount block give returning shoppers a shortcut, and the shop listing can be sorted by newest. I organised product listings by category and gave shoppers search and filtering, so a customer can narrow by type or brand. The site also carries buying guides and tech articles, warranty terms, an FAQ, an about page and contact details. A WhatsApp button and a call button stay fixed on the screen on mobile.

## Limits and what I did not measure

My work was design and implementation; I did not run the shop or its inventory. I built no analytics or order tracking, so I have no traffic, order, conversion or revenue figures, and I do not infer any from how the site looks. Prices, stock, warranty terms and delivery offers are the shop's own and change over time. When I checked the live site, it also advertised specialised phone repair, which suggests the current scope is a little wider than the store described here; that service is not something this page can measure. Check the live site before relying on a specific claim.
